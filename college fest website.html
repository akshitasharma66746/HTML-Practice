Events of the festival:

Coding Competition

Welcome to our high-energy coding competition, where developers, students, and problem-solvers from all backgrounds come together to test their algorithmic skills, logic, and creativity against the clock. Participants will face a series of challenging real-world programming and data-structure problems, competing to write the most efficient, optimized code under strict time constraints. Whether you are a seasoned competitive programmer or a passionate newcomer looking to push your limits, this event offers an exciting platform to learn, collaborate, network with peers, and win great prizes and recognition.

Dance competiton

A dance competition is a vibrant and high-energy event where individuals or groups showcase their technical skill, creativity, and passion across styles like hip-hop, contemporary, and classical before a panel of expert judges. Set against the hum of backstage preparation and the dazzle of stage lights, dancers pour months of grueling practice into a single routine, blending synchronization, emotion, and storytelling to captivate the audience and compete for top honors.

Singing Competition

A singing competition is a vibrant, high-energy event where vocalists of all backgrounds step into the spotlight to showcase their pitch, tone, and stage presence before a panel of expert judges and an enthusiastic audience. Whether featuring soulful ballads, classical ragas, or upbeat pop anthems, the contest transforms the stage into a canvas of raw emotion and artistic expression, pushing participants through intense rounds of preparation, fierce yet friendly rivalry, and unforgettable musical moments that celebrate the universal language of song.

Photography competition

Welcome to our annual photography competition, a nationwide platform designed to celebrate the art of visual storytelling and the unique perspectives of photographers from all walks of life. Whether you capture candid human moments, sweeping natural landscapes, or intricate abstract details, this contest invites you to freeze a fleeting fragment of time and share your distinct vision with the world. Open to both amateur and professional shutterbugs alike, the competition aims to honor technical skill, emotional resonance, and creative originality. Prepare your best frames, make sure your stories unfold through your lens, and submit your work for a chance to win exclusive prizes, public exhibition, and recognition.

Quiz Competition

An exciting and well-organized quiz competition was held in the school auditorium, bringing together enthusiastic teams to test their knowledge across multiple engaging rounds such as general knowledge, science, sports, and a fast-paced rapid-fire session. With a lively quiz master guiding the flow, the participants displayed impressive quick thinking and teamwork, while the audience cheered them on with loud applause. The event concluded with the winning team being awarded trophies and certificates, making it a memorable and knowledge-enriching experience for everyone present.
